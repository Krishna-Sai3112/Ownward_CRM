## Open a workspace

Use the Ownward Labs workspace URL provided by your organization administrator. Sign-in methods, available features, and access policies are configured for that instance.

If you are setting up a new self-hosted instance, follow the [Docker Compose deployment guide](/developers/self-host/capabilities/docker-compose) first. Once the application is running, open its configured `SERVER_URL` in your browser and complete the sign-in flow enabled by the administrator.

## Configure your workspace

After signing in, use the workspace settings to shape Ownward Labs around your team's data and processes.

### 1. Review the data model

Explore the available objects, fields, and relationships. Add or adjust fields to reflect the information your team needs to manage.

[Data Model reference →](/user-guide/data-model/overview)

### 2. Organize your records

Create views and configure the fields shown to your team. Views can help make the records and work most relevant to each role easier to find.

[Views and pipelines →](/user-guide/views-pipelines/overview)

### 3. Connect your processes

Review the integrations and workflow options available in your instance. Connected services and permissions vary with the instance configuration.

[Workflows →](/user-guide/workflows/overview)
