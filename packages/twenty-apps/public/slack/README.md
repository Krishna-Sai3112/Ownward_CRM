# Slack

**Your CRM, in the conversation. Ask Ownward Labs anything from Slack, and post back to any channel.**

## How it works

Mention `@Ownward Labs` in a channel or send it a direct message. It looks up records in your workspace, makes the changes you ask for and replies in the thread.

It only answers people whose Slack account is linked to a Ownward Labs user, and it acts with that user's permissions, so it can't see or change anything they couldn't in Ownward Labs.

> Answers are generated by AI and can be inaccurate. Check important details in Ownward Labs before acting on them.

- **Ask questions**: look up a contact, a company or your open deals.
- **Create and update records**: people, companies, opportunities, notes and tasks, from a message.
- **Preview record links**: paste a link to a Ownward Labs record and Slack shows a preview of it.
- **Slack steps for your workflows**: post, update or delete messages, send ephemerals, add reactions, list channels.

## What you can ask

- “How many open opportunities do we have?”
- “Which deals are closing this month?”
- “What do we know about Acme Corp?”
- “Create a company called Acme Corp”
- “Add a note to Jane Doe: follow up on pricing next week”
- “Create a task to send Acme the proposal by Friday”

## Getting started

1. **Install the app**: click **Install** to add the Slack app to your Ownward Labs workspace.
2. **Connect Slack**: in the app's **Connections** tab, click **Add connection** and approve the install in Slack.
3. **Link your accounts**: Slack accounts with the same email as a Ownward Labs user are linked automatically. An admin can link the rest from the app's **Settings** tab, and the person approves it in a DM from the bot.
4. **Start asking**: type `/invite @Ownward Labs` in a channel and mention it with your question, or send it a DM.

**Self-hosting?** Your admin creates the Slack app and sets its credentials first: see the [self-hosting setup guide](https://github.com/Krishna-Sai3112/Ownward_CRM/blob/main/packages/twenty-apps/public/slack/SETUP.md).

## Billing

**Free to run**: no per-seat, per-message or per-event charge. Assistant answers use AI credits on the model's token usage.
